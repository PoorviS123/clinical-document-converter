## Ambiguities / caveats

- "Heading (No TOC)" is both a field label (title page, synopsis) and a sub-heading (10.7.x, estimands). The inventory labels the body ones `label_heading` with parent number; ids use `v10:<parent>.label.<slug>` and are the least stable ids (titles hold placeholders like "[X]"). Prefer attaching their content to the parent numbered id when no better rule exists.
- Template headings written with placeholders ("[Blinding, Masking]", "Analyses Supporting Secondary Objective [label]") have titles unlike any real protocol; match by number first then fuzzy title, and expect section 9 numbers to differ.
- Title-page "Brief Title"/"Protocol Title" etc. appear twice (title page and 1.1) with distinct ids (title_page.* vs 1.1.synopsis.*); `Regulatory Agency Identifier Number(s)` also.
- `Brief Summary:` in 1.1 is Normal style in the template; `Study Phase: [study phase]` and `[Manufacturer]: [insert manufacturer]` are label-style paragraphs with the value inline.
- The template contains CPT_Example paragraphs that look like headings (for example body 625, "4.2.1.1 Obtaining Study Participant Feedback (Voluntary):" typed manually); they are excluded (not heading styles).
- Fuzzy diff matching is one-to-one greedy; borderline pairs (for example 10.6 "Liver Safety" vs "Liver and Muscle Safety", similarity 0.5) are listed as renamed because they share a number.
- source_index counts every body child including bookmarkEnd, sdt and sectPr elements (same as extract_v10_section1.py).
- The template TOC cached text was the ground truth for numbering; it does not list Heading 4 so L4 numbers (10) are computed only.
