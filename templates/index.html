How a run works

  1. No AIExtract and place

    Text, tables and figures go into the v011 structure. Each value keeps track of its source. Gaps stay as placeholders.

  2. AIAI drafting

    An LLM drafts the sections v011 needs from the protocol's own text. Drafted text is highlighted.

  3. No AIOutputs

    The v011 Word document, a FHIR bundle built by rules, and a report of what was mapped, drafted or left out.

  4. ChecksValidate and review

    ICH M11 and CeSHarP checks run, then you review on the desk.
